STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: SOP-302
TITLE: Thermal Power Plant Boiler & Boiler Tube Safety Inspection
REVISION: 4.1 (Air-Gapped Confidential Document)

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This document defines mandatory safety and inspection protocols for superheater tube pressure boundaries, high-pressure steam turbines, and coal pulverizer units at Thermal Plant Alpha.

2. PRE-INSPECTION SAFETY CHECKLIST
- Ensure main steam isolation valves (MSIV-101 and MSIV-102) are double-blocked and bled.
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) key lockbox #402 must be verified by shift charge engineer.
- Ambient temperature in boiler drum must be strictly below 40°C before human entry.
- Oxygen levels inside enclosed pressure vessels must measure between 19.5% and 23.5%.

3. FREQUENCY & PARAMETERS
- Superheater wall thickness ultrasonics: Every 1,000 operational hours.
- Feedwater pump pressure threshold: Max allowed differential 185 bar.
- Vibration baseline on Bearing 3: Warning at 4.5 mm/s RMS; Emergency Shutdown at 7.1 mm/s RMS.

4. EMERGENCY CONTINGENCY
In case of sudden pressure drop across reheater section (> 15 bar/min), initiate manual trip MT-01 immediately and alert central control room.
