MRPL CONFIDENTIAL OPERATIONAL SOP #SOP-2026-VALVE-09
TITLE: High-Pressure Valve Shutdown Protocol

1. SAFETY THRESHOLD:
If emergency flare line pressure exceeds 115.4 kPa, immediately activate isolation valve IV-402.

2. TIMING SPECIFICATION:
Isolation valve IV-402 must achieve 100% seal within 2.5 seconds of signal trigger.

3. REPORTING REQUIREMENTS:
Log incident to MRPL Air-Gapped Workbench and flag for Chief Engineer approval.
